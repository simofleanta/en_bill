Romania's
power bill

Electricity spending per household, by region.

Of the eight development regions, every one paid more in Q4 2025 than a year earlier. The rise ranged from +28% to +53%. Nationally, monthly spending went from 140 to 197 lei (+41%). That is about 6.5 lei a day, on average.

Outlook

Energy Ministry: +5% minimum in 2027.
AEI: up to +20% this winter, worst case.
Analysts: +10–15% for new contracts.
Energy Ministry (2023): 350k prosumers by 2030 — already passed in 2026, four years early (may count firms differently; unconfirmed).

Average monthly household spending on electricity

1.

Mostly the cap, a little the VAT

Cap ended 1 Jul 2025. VAT also rose 19%→21% a month later, adding about +1.7% on its own — the rest of the Q3–Q4 jump is the cap and market price. Bills were already +9–10% above 2024 in Q1–Q2, before either change.

2.

North-East rose most

+53%, from 127 to 194 lei a month — starting from the country's second-lowest spending.

3.

South-West Oltenia rose least

+28%, from 147 to 189 lei. It had been among the more expensive regions before.

4.

Consumption, unconfirmed for 2025

No kWh price here, and consumption data (2015–2024 panel below) stops before the cap ended; next Eurostat update is ~mid-2027. Appliance ownership did shift a lot over 16 years (cars +84%), but that is a national trend, not evidence for this regional jump.

5.

No regional device data

INS publishes durable goods nationally only. Spending is regional, ownership is not — so the two are never mixed here.
